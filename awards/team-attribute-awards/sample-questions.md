# Sample Questions

## Engineering Inspiration Award

* How has your team advanced respect for engineering in your community?
* What programs or events do you run to promote STEM beyond your own team?
* How do you recruit new members, especially students who may not already see themselves as engineers?
* What partnerships have you built with local companies, schools, or organizations?
* How do those partnerships benefit both your team and the broader community?
* How has your outreach grown or evolved over time?
* How do you measure the impact of your outreach efforts?
* How do you ensure your team culture encourages appreciation for engineering, not just participation in robotics?
* What long-term impact do you hope your team has on your community?

## Gracious Professionalism Award

* What does gracious professionalism mean to your team in practice?
* How do you handle conflict within the team?
* How do you divide work to ensure fairness and inclusion?
* Can you share an example of helping another team at an event?
* How do you respond when things do not go your way in a match?
* How do mentors and students collaborate respectfully?
* What expectations do you set for team conduct at competitions?
* How do you ensure every member feels valued and heard?
* Can you describe a challenging situation and how your team handled it positively?

## Imagery Award in honor of Jack Kamen

* What is your team’s theme, and how did you choose it?
* How is your theme reflected in your robot design?
* How is your team branding consistent across uniforms, pit, robot, and media?
* What design choices were intentional, and why?
* How do aesthetics and function complement each other on your robot?
* How do new members learn and maintain your team’s visual identity?
* What story does your team’s imagery tell?
* How does your pit design reinforce your overall theme?

## Judges Award

* What is something unique about your team that you are especially proud of?
* Is there a program, tradition, or initiative that sets your team apart?
* What challenge has your team overcome this season?
* What impact are you making that might not fit into a traditional award category?
* How has your team grown this year beyond just building a robot?
* What would you want judges to remember most about your team?

## Rookie All-Star Award

* What does FIRST mean to your team?
* How are you building a strong foundation within your school or organization?
* How did you recruit your first members?
* What outreach have you completed in your rookie season?
* How are you building relationships with sponsors?
* What systems are you putting in place to ensure your team lasts beyond this year?
* How do students take ownership of the team?
* What have you learned about FIRST culture and values so far?
* How are you preparing to grow in future seasons?

## Rookie Inspiration Award

* How are you inspiring respect for engineering in your community as a rookie team?
* What outreach have you completed in your first season?
* How did you attract new members who may not have prior STEM experience?
* How are you promoting science and technology education at your school?
* What feedback have you received from your community about your team?
* How are you building excitement about engineering among younger students?
* What goals do you have for expanding your impact next year?

## Team Spirit Award

* How does your team show enthusiasm at competitions?
* How do you promote unity within your team?
* What traditions or cheers are unique to your team?
* How do you represent FIRST in your school and community?
* How do you balance competitive intensity with positive energy?
* How does your team maintain morale during a tough event?
* How do your appearance and branding reflect your team pride?
* How do you include all members in showing team spirit?

## Team Sustainability Award

* How do you recruit and retain members year over year?
* What is your plan for leadership transition?
* How do you manage financial risk?
* What are your primary revenue sources?
* How do you ensure consistent funding?
* What is your long-term action plan for growth?
* How do you track and evaluate progress toward team goals?
* How do mentors support sustainability without limiting student leadership?
* What systems are in place to ensure the team thrives beyond current members?
